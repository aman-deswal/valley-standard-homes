# Starter backlog

These are the recommended seed issues for the Valley Standard operations board. They intentionally avoid duplicating work already done in the prior planning session: brand selection, initial website launch, domain setup, and starting the OREA application.

## Foundation

### Form Valley Standard Property Management LLC

- **Labels:** `area: compliance`, `type: task`, `priority: high`
- **Launch phase:** Foundation
- **Why:** The prior plan identified LLC formation as the highest-leverage next step because it unlocks EIN, banking, insurance quotes, city licensing, and clean operating identity.
- **Definition of done:** LLC filing is submitted or completed, confirmation is saved, and any exact legal-name implications for OREA are noted.

### Obtain EIN for the LLC

- **Labels:** `area: banking`, `area: compliance`, `type: task`, `priority: high`
- **Launch phase:** Foundation
- **Dependency:** LLC filing
- **Definition of done:** EIN is issued, saved securely, and available for banking, insurance, tax, and vendor setup.

### Open business operating account

- **Labels:** `area: banking`, `type: task`, `priority: high`
- **Launch phase:** Foundation
- **Dependency:** LLC and EIN
- **Definition of done:** Operating account is open, funding approach is clear, and recurring business expenses can be paid from the account.

### Build first-year launch budget

- **Labels:** `area: finance-pricing`, `type: task`, `priority: medium`
- **Launch phase:** Foundation
- **Definition of done:** Budget includes licensing, course/exam, LLC, city license, insurance, software, website/email, bookkeeping, networking, and launch marketing.
