# Business awareness and website direction

This document distills the current Valley Standard Homes source of truth into durable business context for future website, content, and operations work. It should be read before changing public copy, issue templates, onboarding materials, or launch workflows.

## Business core

Valley Standard Homes is a pre-launch, founder-led single-family property management business for the Portland area. The business is being built around a more personal standard of care for owners who want fewer loose ends, clearer communication, and local follow-through for the home they own.

**Primary business promise:** Not just managed. Looked after.

**Operating line:** Clear systems behind the scenes. Human communication out front.

**Launch posture:** Coming Fall 2026. Until the Oregon property manager license is confirmed active, public conversion paths should say "Join the owner interest list," "Ask a question," "Get launch updates," or similar pre-launch language.

## Primary audiences

1. Out-of-state owners who need trusted local eyes on a Portland-area home.
2. Busy local owners who want fewer tenant, vendor, and maintenance interruptions.
3. Accidental landlords caring for a former home or first investment property.
4. Single-family investors who value steady systems, communication, and property care.

## Website north star

The site should build toward a calm, local, owner-focused resource hub rather than a generic national property-management template. It should make the owner feel that a real person with direct ownership experience is applying disciplined systems to the everyday realities of caring for a single-family rental home.

**Visual direction:** deep evergreen, white, cool mist gray, restrained rust, editorial serif headlines, readable sans serif body copy, generous spacing, thin rules, real Portland-area home photography, and authentic founder imagery.

**Content direction:** owner-first, locally specific, process-backed, real proof only, and one clear action per page.

## Guardrails

Do not publish or imply:

- "Hire us now," "Start management today," "Now accepting properties," or "licensed property manager" before confirmed license status.
- Pricing, reviews, client counts, portfolio details, availability, response times, guarantees, public address, phone number, office hours, license number, or market statistics unless supplied and approved.
- Testimonials, ratings, review schema, or family-member review language unless exact approved words are supplied.
- Local office claims, fake map pins, or city-specific claims that imply a physical location in every service area.
- LocalBusiness structured data that would require a fabricated or private address.

Use Organization structured data with confirmed properties only until the business has an approved public location and compliance guidance supports a change.

## Website build roadmap

1. **Foundation and homepage:** strengthen the current homepage, add reusable SEO basics, sitemap, robots, Organization JSON-LD, and pre-launch conversion clarity.
2. **Services:** build the services overview and six dedicated service pages with distinct owner problems, operating sequences, communication expectations, FAQs, related links, and pre-launch CTAs.
3. **Areas served:** build the area hub and six distinct owner-facing area pages. Avoid city-name swaps and unsupported local facts.
4. **Trust, resources, and conversion:** build About, Contact, Privacy, Resources, and publish the three launch guides supplied in the handoff.
5. **Onboarding and launch QA:** produce owner and resident onboarding packet templates, validate accessibility and performance, connect approved forms/analytics/Search Console, and complete launch QA.
